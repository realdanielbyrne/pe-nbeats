\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The gain this paper reports is position on the accuracy-against-size
frontier, not accuracy: two significant losses on M4 and an outright
loss on Traffic-96, 29.37 sMAPE at 1.81M parameters against 26.24 at
52.3M. On the minimal frontier rows two differences reach significance
against us, both favouring the baseline, the larger being Daily at
$+0.048$ sMAPE; we print them rather than argue with them.

Several scopes bound the rest. All comparisons are single models; the
published N-BEATS results are 180-model ensembles, and whether
ensembling exploits the excess trunk capacity is untested. The
trunk-width sweep covers Yearly and Monthly in an interpretable
configuration only. The backcast-path family equivalence is empirical
rather than proven, since truncated bases from different families span
different subspaces that no rotation relates. The evidence base is
M4-centric, scoped to forecasting alone, and built on seed budgets of
eight to ten per cell, so effects smaller than those spreads are below
our resolution; the one Tourism significance we lean on, Quarterly width,
is uncorrected. On Traffic-96 the boundary of the narrow-trunk default
is open. An earlier attempt to measure it was abandoned as invalid, a
configuration error of ours inflated the seed spread roughly tenfold,
and its five completed runs are not cited by anyone for any purpose;
the rerun under the reference schedule is the single most useful
experiment left, until which the boundary stays open here.

Three committed experiments did not land before this revision and are
disclosed exactly as they were disclosed in review. The AE-trunk row of
the two-by-two crossing $\{\text{Fourier}, \text{wavelet}\} \times
\{\text{linear}, \text{AE}\}$ at the matched budget was committed in
response to a reviewer's question and is unrun; the linear row stands
alone. The sweep of the intermediate taper width at fixed bottleneck
size was committed and is unrun; adjacent evidence suggests it will not
rescue the taper, but that is inference from other nulls, not a
measurement. The weight-decay arm separating regularization from
parameter count was committed and is unrun. Each is queued, none is
quietly dropped, and no claim in this paper depends on their outcome.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

N-BEATS carries roughly fifty times more trunk capacity than its
single-model accuracy requires, and replacing each unconstrained
synthesis head with a frozen orthonormal basis turns that observation
into a design space: the rank becomes an explicit knob, and two
quantities computable before training --- captured energy and effective
rank --- say how accurate a truncation will be and whether it will train.
At complete rank basis identity is provably inert, and the identity
matrix that several submitted headline cells silently used is the
limiting case that corroborates the proof. Instability turned out to be
negative forecasts scored at sMAPE's ceiling, removable by construction
on non-negative targets. The resulting preset holds out of sample on
Tourism within its registered margin at a fraction of the parameters,
and where it loses, on Traffic-96, the loss is printed beside the wins.
The compression came from the trunk, the accuracy comes from the
frontier position, and the paper now claims exactly that.
