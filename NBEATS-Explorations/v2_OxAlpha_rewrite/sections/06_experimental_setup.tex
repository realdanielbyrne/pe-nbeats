\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Datasets}
\label{sec:setup:data}

M4 \citep{makridakis2020m4} is the primary benchmark: six frequencies,
100{,}000 series, lookback $L = 5H$, and the sMAPE loss, with the
originally published accuracy reachable through the overall weighted
average. The six periods and horizons are Yearly $H = 6$, Quarterly
$H = 8$, Monthly $H = 18$, Weekly $H = 13$, Daily $H = 14$, and Hourly
$H = 48$; Monthly is $H = 18$ throughout this paper. Three
out-of-distribution datasets complement M4. Tourism contributes
1{,}311 mixed-frequency series in a column-oriented layout at horizons
$H \in \{4, 8, 24\}$, again scored by sMAPE. Weather-96 provides
21 channels of 10-minute observations with a horizon of 96 under an MSE
loss, and Traffic-96 provides 862 hourly sensor series at the same
horizon, also under MSE. A single univariate Milk production series
($H = 6$) serves as the small-data stress test for convergence
behaviour. Table~\ref{tab:datasets} summarizes the datasets.

\subsection{Training protocols}
\label{sec:setup:protocols}

Every architecture runs under three protocols. The paper-sample faithful
protocol follows \citet{oreshkin2020nbeats}: iteration-sampled training
windows honour the in-sample constraint $L_h \cdot H$ ($L_h = 1.5$ on
Yearly and Quarterly, $L_h = 10$ elsewhere), Adam trains at a learning
rate of $10^{-3}$ with batch size 1024 for a budget of $10^{5}$ gradient
steps, the learning rate halves at ten evenly spaced milestones, weights
are shared within each stack, each stack carries one block, and the
forecast multiplier is 5. Validation runs every 100 steps, and early
stopping monitors validation sMAPE with patience 20 val-check units and
a minimum improvement of $10^{-3}$; without the sub-epoch cadence,
stopping fires on the first warmup-corrupted check. The plateau variant
is identical except that \texttt{ReduceLROnPlateau} (factor 0.5,
patience 3 val-check units, cooldown 1, minimum learning rate
$10^{-6}$, monitored on validation loss) replaces the fixed milestone
schedule. The third protocol is sliding-window sampling with a cosine
schedule, which produces the strongest absolute scores on the
long-horizon periods; absolute sMAPE is never compared across sampling
protocols, so every leaderboard in this paper is protocol-local.

The two paper-sample variants exist as a schedule control. Gains that
appear under both schedulers are robust evidence; gains under the
faithful scheduler only are the head-to-head replacement claim; gains
under the plateau scheduler only are reported as schedule-contingent
rather than architectural. Per-period scheduler verdicts are in
Table~\ref{tab:lr_per_period}, protocol verdicts in
Table~\ref{tab:protocol_per_period}, and the complete hyperparameter
surface in Table~\ref{tab:hyperparams_defaults}.

\subsection{Sweep design}
\label{sec:setup:sweeps}

The submitted evidence base was a 112-configuration comprehensive sweep
under the sliding-window protocol, spanning five groups: the paper
baselines, homogeneous frozen-basis-plus-trend stacks in the unified
layout, the same two ingredients in alternating stacks, AE-trunk
efficiency variants, and generic controls; the grids are enumerated in
Table~\ref{tab:sweep_grids}. A curated 53-configuration subset was then
rerun under both paper-sample variants. The output-activation extension
was swept separately, $\{\text{off}, \text{forecast-head-only}\}$, so
that the extension knob never confounds an architectural comparison;
paper-faithful claims exclude the marked rows throughout.

Roughly 1{,}865 further runs were added after submission, and every
load-bearing number they produced appears in this paper with its test
and sample size: a 240-run basis-identity grid over six periods at ten
seeds; a 160-run trunk-width sweep at ten seeds per rung; a 420-run
comparison against the DWT-preprocessing design of
\citet{pramanick2024fusion}; a 240-run out-of-sample validation of the
compact preset on Tourism; and the divergence-mechanism and
nonlinearity-placement studies. Nine registered predictions failed and
are reported as failures in the appendix register rather than absorbed
silently.

\subsection{Seeds, statistics, and markers}
\label{sec:setup:stats}

Cells default to ten random seeds, and every printed score is a mean
$\pm$ standard deviation over surviving seeds with the per-cell count
stated. The health filter excludes a run whose divergence flag is set,
whose sMAPE exceeds 100, or which stopped at epoch zero with sMAPE above
50; surviving counts of nine or ten are typical. Pairwise comparisons
within a configuration use the Wilcoxon signed-rank test on matched
seeds; comparisons between arms use two-sided Mann--Whitney $U$ with
$n \geq 8$ per cell; multi-rung sweeps use Kruskal--Wallis; counts of
budget-matched wins use the sign test. All $p$-values are raw, and an
uncorrected value is labelled as such wherever multiplicity would
matter. The smallest exact permutation $p$ attainable with five bases is
0.017, and the Traffic-96 capture-law statistic is quoted in exactly
that form: basis-level Spearman $\rho = -1.0$, exact $p = 0.017$ at
$n = 5$ bases, with the run-level correlation $\rho = -0.98$ over 25
runs at $p = 9.0 \times 10^{-18}$.

Equivalence claims are bounded rather than merely unrejected wherever
the bound exists. The trunk-width result is the model case: the five
rung means span 0.098 sMAPE while the seed spread inside any single rung
is 0.15--0.25, so the rung-mean analysis bounds the whole effect below
the noise floor of one cell.

Three markers appear in every results table and are defined formally in
the unified legend of Section~\ref{sec:method}. Here we recall them.
The dagger marks the forecast-head output activation, an extension
beyond \citet{oreshkin2020nbeats}. Superscript g marks a historically
gated AE trunk, retained for provenance now that the gate is shown
null. Double dagger marks a cell whose frozen basis, at that family and
horizon, degenerated to the identity matrix, so the label ``wavelet''
does not apply to it; the identity disclosure is explained inline where
the affected cells appear.
